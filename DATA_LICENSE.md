# Data licence
The BanglaOR texts (trigger and neutral sets in all five forms), the translations, the reference labels, the
translation ratings and the model outputs in this repository are released under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They are released for evaluating and improving safety systems, not for training models to evade them.
Third-party data (MultiJail; the Romim et al. Bengali comment corpus) is not included and keeps its own licence.
